# AI Lead Machine Agent

An enterprise-grade AI-powered lead generation and cold email automation system built with n8n, Google Gemini AI, and Gmail.

This workflow automatically discovers business email addresses, generates personalized outreach emails using AI, and delivers them through Gmail with minimal human intervention.

---

# Overview

The AI Lead Machine Agent streamlines the complete outbound lead generation process by combining AI-powered data extraction, intelligent email generation, and automated email delivery.

Instead of manually searching for business contacts and writing cold emails, this workflow performs the entire process automatically.

---

# Key Features

- AI-powered lead generation
- Business email extraction from company websites
- Google Gemini AI integration
- Personalized cold email generation
- Gmail automation
- Dynamic email tone selection
- Automated lead processing
- Scalable outreach workflow
- Enterprise-ready architecture

---

# Workflow Process

1. User submits business information through an n8n Form.
2. The workflow validates the submitted data.
3. Google Gemini extracts the most relevant business email address from the company's website.
4. AI generates a personalized cold email based on the selected email style.
5. Required email fields are prepared automatically.
6. Gmail sends the personalized outreach email.
7. The workflow repeats the process for multiple leads automatically.
