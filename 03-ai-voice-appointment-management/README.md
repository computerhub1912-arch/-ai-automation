# AI Voice Appointment Management System

> **Enterprise-grade AI-powered voice receptionist built with n8n, Vapi AI, Google Calendar, and Airtable for intelligent appointment scheduling, rescheduling, cancellation, and real-time workflow automation.**

---

# Overview

The **AI Voice Appointment Management System** is an enterprise-level workflow automation solution that enables businesses to manage appointments entirely through natural voice conversations.

Powered by **Vapi AI** and orchestrated with **n8n**, the system functions as a virtual receptionist capable of understanding customer requests, checking calendar availability, scheduling appointments, updating existing bookings, cancelling reservations, and synchronizing all records across Google Calendar and Airtable.

Designed for production environments, the workflow eliminates repetitive manual scheduling tasks while delivering a seamless customer experience through intelligent voice interactions.
